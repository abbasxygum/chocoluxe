Our kitchen

A small counter, a big obsession

Chocoluxe started the way most good things do — with one tempering bowl, a second hand, and a stubborn belief that chocolate should be made the slow way. We still work that way.

Every morning the team melts couverture, folds in the fillings, and hands-rolls the bonbons while they're still warm. We only make what the order book asks for, which is why some things run out and some things only appear for a season.

  • Single-origin couverture, roasted in-house
  • No palm fat, no artificial flavouring, ever
  • Rolled and wrapped by the same four people who mix it

— Iram
